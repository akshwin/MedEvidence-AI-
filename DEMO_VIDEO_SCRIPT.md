# 🎬 MedEvidence AI Demo Video Script

**Duration**: 2-3 minutes  
**Style**: Screen recording with voiceover  
**Target Audience**: Healthcare professionals, researchers, medical students

3. Highlight citations section
4. Click on a citation to show it expands with paper details
5. Show PubMed link

> "Each citation includes the paper title, authors, journal, publication year, and a direct link to PubMed. You can verify every claim by accessing the original research."

**Voiceover**:

**Voiceover (while loading)**:

## Visual Guidelines

### Screen Recording Tips:
1. **Resolution**: Record in 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum
2. **Frame Rate**: 30fps or 60fps
3. **Cursor**: Use a visible cursor, or highlight clicks with circles/animations
4. **Zoom**: Use zoom effects to highlight important UI elements
5. **Transitions**: Smooth transitions between sections
6. **Speed**: Use 1.5x speed for loading screens (if needed)

### UI Elements to Highlight:
- ✅ Logo and branding
- ✅ "30M+ Papers" statistic
- ✅ Chat input and send button
- ✅ Answer formatting and structure
- ✅ Citations with expandable details
- ✅ PDF download button
- ✅ Search tab and results
- ✅ Paper cards with abstracts
- ✅ Theme toggle
- ✅ Responsive design

### Color Scheme:
- Match the app's emerald/teal color scheme
- Use consistent branding colors
- Ensure good contrast for visibility

---

## Voiceover Guidelines

### Voice Style:
- **Tone**: Professional, confident, friendly
- **Pace**: Clear and moderate (not rushed)
- **Accent**: Neutral/clear pronunciation
- **Energy**: Enthusiastic but professional

### Recording Tips:
1. Use a quality microphone (USB mic or better)
2. Record in a quiet environment
3. Use a pop filter to reduce plosives
4. Record at 48kHz sample rate
5. Normalize audio levels
6. Remove background noise in post-production

### Script Variations:
- Can be shortened to 2 minutes for shorter attention spans
- Can be extended to 4 minutes with more detailed examples
- Add pauses for emphasis on key features
