## 💡 **Naming Tips**

1. **Check domain availability** first
2. **Check trademark conflicts** (especially in healthcare)
3. **Test with target customers** (doctors, hospital admins)
4. **Consider SEO** (what will people search for?)
5. **Keep it simple** (easy to spell, say, remember)
